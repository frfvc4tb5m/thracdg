Internal Medicine

An 8-day late period is often not a cause for concern, but the reason depends on several factors. Common causes include stress, recent lifestyle or weight changes, hormonal shifts, thyroid issues, PCOS, perimenopause, or pregnancy. Tracking your typical cycle length and noting any recent changes can help identify the likely cause.

Explanation

If your period is 8 days late, it's sometimes just a sign that your cycle is a bit off, which can happen because of stress, changes in weight, or a shift in your normal routine. Many women experience occasional delays even when their cycles are usually regular. If you are sexually active, it might be a good idea to take a pregnancy test to rule that out. In some cases, changes in your body or even the use of period-tracking apps can help you see that a short delay is not unusual. However, if you notice that your period becomes consistently late or you experience other symptoms like severe pain or unusual bleeding, you can check your symptoms with our free AI-powered tool to better understand what might be going on. They can check your hormone levels or look for other causes, and help you decide if any further steps or treatments are needed.
