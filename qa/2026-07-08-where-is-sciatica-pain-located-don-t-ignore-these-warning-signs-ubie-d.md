Sciatica is nerve pain that typically begins in the lower back and radiates through the buttock and down the back of the leg. It occurs when the sciatic nerve—the longest nerve in the body—becomes compressed or irritated, often due to a herniated disc, bone spur, or spinal stenosis. Symptoms can range from a mild ache to sharp, burning pain, and may include tingling, numbness, or weakness in the affected leg.
