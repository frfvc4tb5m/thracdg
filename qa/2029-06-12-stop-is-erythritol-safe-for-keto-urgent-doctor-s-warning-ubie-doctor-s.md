Is erythritol safe for keto or low-carb diets?

Erythritol is a sugar alcohol widely regarded as safe for keto and low-carb diets because it does not raise blood sugar or insulin levels. It is a type of sugar alcohol, which means it provides sweetness without adding many calories or affecting blood sugar levels. This makes it a popular choice for people who want to reduce their sugar intake while maintaining a sweet taste in their foods.
