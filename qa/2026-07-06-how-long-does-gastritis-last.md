There are four known types of gastritis.

Acute Gastritis

Acute gastritis is the most common type and is defined as a sudden onset of inflammation in the lining of the stomach. This type is usually caused by things like drinking too much alcohol or certain foods or irritants.

Chronic Gastritis

Chronic gastritis is an ongoing condition that is typically caused by a more serious illness, such as some infections and autoimmune conditions. Finding permanent relief for symptoms can be difficult and usually requires seeing a medical specialist.

Erosive Gastritis

Erosive is a more severe and often acute form of gastritis that can cause lesions and ulcers in the stomach lining. This causes symptoms such as bleeding, but the extent of the damage is difficult to detect without an endoscopic examination.

Atrophic Gastritis

Atrophic gastritis is a specific type of gastritis that not only causes inflammation, but thinning of the stomach lining as the cells in the stomach lining begin to mimic those of the intestines. This is a chronic condition that requires specialized treatment.
