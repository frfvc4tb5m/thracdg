About the Symptom

It describes the the separation of a fingernail or toenail from its pink nail bed, also known as onycholysis. The separation occurs gradually and is painless. It is diagnosed by the whitish appearance of the separated nail plate from the nail bed. In fingers, the majority is caused by trauma, manicuring, occupational or self-induced behavior. The most common disease producing fingernail onycholysis is psoriasis. Certain medications, including chemotherapy, can also produce finger onycholysis.Toenail onycholysis has a very different etiology. It is mechanical, the result of pressure on the toes from the closed shoes, while walking.
