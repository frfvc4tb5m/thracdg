Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Urgent care visits without insurance typically cost between $100 and $200 out-of-pocket, though prices can range higher depending on the services needed. This is significantly less than an emergency room visit, which often costs $1,000 or more for similar non-life-threatening issues. Factors influencing the final bill include the clinic's location, the complexity of your condition, and any additional tests, labs, or procedures required during your visit.
