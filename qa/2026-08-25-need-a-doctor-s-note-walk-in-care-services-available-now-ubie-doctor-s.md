Internal Medicine

Walk-in clinics provide convenient, no-appointment medical care for non-emergency health concerns. Common services include:

Treatment for minor illnesses: colds, flu, sore throats, ear infections, urinary tract infections, and mild allergic reactions

Care for minor injuries: sprains, minor cuts, burns, and simple fractures

Preventive care: vaccinations, flu shots, and routine health screenings

Diagnostic services: basic lab tests, strep tests, and physical exams

Walk-in clinics are ideal when your primary care doctor is unavailable but your condition isn't severe enough for the emergency room. However, knowing whether your symptoms truly warrant a walk-in visit—or something more urgent—can be tricky. Before heading out, take a free, instant, online symptom check to better understand what may be causing your symptoms and get guidance on the most appropriate next step. It takes just a few minutes and could save you time, money, and unnecessary worry.
