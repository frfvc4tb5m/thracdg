Men may experience leaking after peeing, known as post-micturition dribble. This is when some urine leaks out after finishing urination.

Possible Causes

Post-Micturition Dribble: According to research, post-micturition dribble can occur due to the way urine is left in the urethra after peeing. Sometimes, the muscles don't fully squeeze out all the urine, causing a little to leak out afterward.

Surgical Impact: Another study found that surgeries, such as those for prostate issues, can affect urinary control. This can lead to leaking as the body adjusts after surgery.

Urethral Repair: When the urethra is repaired after injury, it can also affect how well the bladder holds urine, leading to possible leakage.
