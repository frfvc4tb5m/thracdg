Explanation

Wrist pain can happen for many reasons, even if there is no fracture. Sometimes, the soft tissues like ligaments can get hurt. Ligament injuries can cause pain and make it hard to move the wrist. Inflammation of the wrist can also make it sore.

Other Possible Causes:

Soft Tissue Injuries: Ligament sprains are common and can cause pain without any visible fracture.

Inflammation: Conditions like tendonitis or arthritis can lead to wrist pain.

Nerve Issues: Sometimes, the nerves in the wrist can get pinched, causing pain.

Imaging and Diagnosis:

Doctors might use MRIs to look inside the wrist and see what is causing the pain. This helps in understanding if the pain is due to soft tissue problems or something else.

Understanding these causes can help in finding the right treatment and making the wrist feel better.
