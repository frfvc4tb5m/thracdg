What are the common causes of developing a big stomach?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A large stomach, or increased belly size, is typically caused by a combination of factors including genetics, hormonal changes, diet, physical inactivity, stress, and poor sleep. These influences contribute to the accumulation of visceral fat—fat stored deep within the abdomen around vital organs—which can pose greater health risks than fat stored elsewhere in the body.

Explanation

Having a big stomach often means there is too much fat inside the belly, called visceral fat. This can happen for a few reasons:

Genetics: Some people are more likely to have a big stomach because of their family history. If your parents or grandparents had more belly fat, you might too.

Hormones: Hormones in your body can also affect how much fat you have in your belly. For example, certain hormones can make your body store more fat in the belly area.

Lifestyle: What you eat and how much you move can also play a big role. Eating a lot of unhealthy foods and not exercising enough can lead to more belly fat.
