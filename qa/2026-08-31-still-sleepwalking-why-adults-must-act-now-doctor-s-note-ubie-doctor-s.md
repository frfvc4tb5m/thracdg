Can sleepwalking continue into adulthood?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sleepwalking can persist into adulthood and, in some cases, begin during adult years. While often associated with childhood, adult-onset sleepwalking is a recognized condition that can be triggered by stress, sleep deprivation, medications, or underlying sleep disorders. The good news: sleepwalking is treatable through a range of approaches, including improved sleep hygiene, stress management, cognitive behavioral therapy, and, in some cases, medication.

Because sleepwalking in adults can signal an underlying health issue, understanding your specific symptoms is an important first step.
