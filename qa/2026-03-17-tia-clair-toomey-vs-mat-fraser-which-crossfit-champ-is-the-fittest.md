# Tia-Clair Toomey vs. Mat Fraser: Which CrossFit Champ Is the Fittest?

[vs Mat](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/1815022411.html)

CrossFit Games head judge Adrian Bozman finished up his duties for the first stage of the 2020 CrossFit Games, and he's still basking in the glory of the intense competition. "It was a blast, I hope you guys had a good time watching it," he says in a video posted by the CrossFit Games channel.

The final stage is set to take place on national television this weekend, airing October 23 through 25. The field has been whittled down to the final five competitors for men and women, with reigning repeat champions and training partners Mat Fraser and Tia-Clair Toomey sitting in the number one position for their respective grouping. No one would be shocked, then, if the champions extended their winning streaks for the fifth and fourth years in a row, respectively, setting new records for dominance in the sport. So Bozman posed a slightly more interesting question: who is fitter, Fraser or Toomey?

While the athletes compete in different divisions—which means they work with slightly different demands for certain events—the judge found smart ways to compare them head to head. Bozman used the athletes' performances at the qualifying round of the Games to determine who performed better, and therefore, who is fitter.

For the Friendly Fran, Fraser won with the better time (3:08). But the 1RM Front Squat was a tie. Bozman figured bodyweight percentage was a fair way to compare them, and it turned out they both got 221 percent, or 2.21 times their bodyweight, with Fraser squatting 425 pounds and Toomey 313 pounds.

"A dead tie on that, which is crazy," says Bozman. While this is a common means to compare big lifts across genders or other categories, it's worth noting that the event didn't have a weight class within the men's or women's division IRL. That means the winner was determined by who got the biggest weight, even if the competitors weren't perfectly matched weight-wise. For the Toomey vs. Fraser purposes, however, the methodology is about as close as you can get to a fair estimation.

Toomey won the Damn Diane event with the best time (2:28). The Max Row also goes to Toomey—but there's some more percentages at play to determine that win.

"We figured that by comparing their times compared to the best world record recorded on the Concept [the rowing machine] for their weight and age category," says Bozman. "Tia stacks up at 109 percent of the world record, and Mat is at 111 percent of the world record, which means that Tia with her time was closer to the world record. So she edged out Mat by a 2 percent faster time."

Fraser takes the Nasty Nancy event with best time (17:50), and the Handstand Hold (1:35) too. But Toomey notches a win with the best time in Awful Annie (8:01).

"That's three wins a piece, and a dead tie on the squat," says Bozman. "Which means they are tied in my book going into Aromas." The conclusion is almost unsurprising, given the consistent level of dominance from both champions.

Have a problem with the result of the head-to-head matchup? Don't go running to Bozman with complaints. "Nobody asked me, I'm just the head judge," he says.

We'll all see if the two champs can keep up their winning ways this weekend, when the 2020 CrossFit Games return for the finals.
