What Are NAD+ Boosters?

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell. It helps:

Convert food into usable energy

Support DNA repair

Regulate key enzymes (sirtuins) involved in aging and metabolism

NAD+ booster supplements aim to raise cellular NAD+ levels. Common forms include:

Nicotinamide riboside (NR)

Understanding Heart Palpitations

Heart palpitations describe sensations such as:

Skipped or extra beats

Fluttering in the chest

Racing or pounding heartbeat

Palpitations can be brief and harmless, or they may signal an underlying issue. Triggers include stress, caffeine, dehydration—and, in some cases, supplements.

Why NAD+ Boosters and Heart Palpitations May Be Linked

The exact connection between NAD+ supplementation and palpitations isn't fully understood. Possible explanations include:

Metabolic shifts: Higher NAD+ levels can boost mitochondrial activity, potentially increasing heart rate.

Sirtuin activation: NAD+ activates sirtuin enzymes that influence cardiovascular regulation.

Purity and additives: Some products contain stimulants, fillers, or untested ingredients.

Individual sensitivity: Genetics, existing heart conditions, or interactions with medications may heighten risk.

Because research is still emerging, any new or unusual heart sensations deserve attention.

Who Is at Higher Risk?

While anyone can experience palpitations, certain factors raise the likelihood:

Pre-existing heart conditions (arrhythmias, valve issues)

High blood pressure or cholesterol

Thyroid dysfunction

Use of multiple supplements or prescription stimulants

High caffeine intake, alcohol, or nicotine use

If you fall into one of these groups, extra caution makes sense.

When to Notify Your Doctor

Pay attention to your body. Notify your healthcare provider if palpitations:

Last more than a few seconds or occur frequently

Are accompanied by dizziness, lightheadedness, chest pain, or shortness of breath

Wake you from sleep or interfere with daily activities

Occur at rest, rather than only during exercise or stress

Even mild, infrequent palpitations warrant a quick check-in. It helps your doctor track patterns and rule out serious issues.

Benefits of Early Notification

Accurate DiagnosisYour doctor can perform tests (EKG, blood work, Holter monitor) to determine if your palpitations stem from a harmless cause or a more serious condition.

Personalized AdviceBased on your health history and current medications, your physician can recommend:

Adjusting or pausing the NAD+ supplement

Switching to a different formulation or brand

Reducing dosage or frequency

Preventing ComplicationsEarly intervention can catch arrhythmias or other heart conditions before they worsen.

Peace of MindUnderstanding the cause of your palpitations reduces uncertainty and lets you continue supplements safely, if appropriate.

Tips for Safe NAD+ Booster Use

Start with the lowest effective dose and increase gradually.

Choose reputable brands that provide third-party testing for purity.

Avoid stacking multiple stimulatory supplements at the same time.

Stay well-hydrated and monitor caffeine intake.

Keep your healthcare provider informed of any changes.

When to Seek Immediate Help

While palpitations alone are not always an emergency, call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department if you experience:

Sudden, severe chest pain or pressure

Fainting or near-syncope

Severe shortness of breath

Confusion or sudden weakness on one side

These symptoms could indicate a heart attack, stroke, or serious arrhythmia.
