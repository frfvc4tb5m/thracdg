HOW DOES EPITHALON WORK? Before we can discuss how Epithalon works, let us focus on our genetic make-up, that is, DNA. DNA is embedded in the nucleus of our cells. At the tips of our DNA are strands called telomeres. Telomeres protect DNA and prevent the loss of genetic information during cell division.

However, each cell division inadvertently shortens telomeres. This shortening can also be exacerbated by oxidative stress. When telomeres shorten to a critical size, the cell grows old, which causes aging as we know it.

However, the body produces an enzyme called telomerase, which combats the degradation of telomeres, promoting their rejuvenation and DNA repair. Younger people produce more telomerase and therefore have longer telomeres. Longer telomeres encourage better cell health and rejuvenation. Older people produce less of this enzyme, which causes cells to grow old and die.
