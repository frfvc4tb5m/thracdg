What is the typical price range for urgent care services?

Urgent care visits typically cost between $100 and $200 for a basic, uninsured appointment. However, the total price can range from $75 to over $500 depending on your location, the services performed (such as X-rays, lab tests, or stitches), and whether you have insurance coverage.
