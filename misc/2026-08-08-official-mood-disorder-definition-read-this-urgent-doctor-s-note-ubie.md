Mood disorders are mental health conditions that primarily affect a person's emotional state, causing persistent extreme feelings such as sadness, hopelessness, or elevated euphoria. Common types include major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, and persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia). These conditions can disrupt sleep, energy, focus, and daily functioning, but they are treatable with therapy, medication, and lifestyle support.

Explanation

Mood disorders are a group of mental health conditions that primarily affect a person's emotional state. People with mood disorders might experience prolonged periods of extreme sadness, known as depression, or excessive euphoria or irritability known as mania. These conditions can significantly impact daily life, making it hard to function normally.

Types of Mood Disorders

Depressive Disorders: These include major depressive disorder, where individuals feel very sad or lose interest in activities they once enjoyed.
