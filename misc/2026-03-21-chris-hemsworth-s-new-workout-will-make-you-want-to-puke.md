# Chris Hemsworth’s New Workout Will Make You Want to Puke

Chris Hemsworth is meddlin' in some dark sided stuff. In a new workout devised by his fellow fitness bros at Centr, the Marvel star's fitness app, and posted to his Instagram, Hemsworth takes on a circuit that would throw Marguerite Perrin into hysterics and leave you feeling like a pile of jelly at the end. Coined the Devil's Workout, Hemsworth and trainer Luke Zocchi have built a brutal little circuit.

It breaks down as follows:

Do six reps of each of the following six moves without rest between each exercise. Rest for a minute. And repeat the circuit five more times for a total of six rounds.
