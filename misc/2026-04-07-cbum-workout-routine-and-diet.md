Who is Chris Bumstead (CBum)

Chris Bumstead (CBum on Instagram) was born in Canada and has risen through the ranks of the IFBB ranks to become the current Mr. Olympia and one of the best bodybuilders ever. Bumstead is known for being quite humble and remains one of the few bodybuilders who delivers year after year.

He started weightlifting when he was only 14 years old, but was always involved in sports. He started competing in bodybuilding by age 19 and earned his Pro Card by age 21 when he won the 2016 IFBB North American Bodybuilding Championship.

Chris Bumstead CBum Workout Plan

Chris Bumstead follows what can be described as a “bro split” and he might change things as needed. For instance, it is not uncommon for him to remove volume from his leg extensions or squats to allow for more arm workout volume on prep.

He will utilize both volume and intensity to get the best results possible depending on how his coach, Hany Rambod, sees fit.
