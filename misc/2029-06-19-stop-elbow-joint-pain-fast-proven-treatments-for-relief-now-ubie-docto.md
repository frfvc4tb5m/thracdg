Explanation

Elbow pain can be treated using different methods. First, doctors usually start with simple steps like resting your arm and using ice to reduce swelling. Medicines such as nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) can help ease pain. For conditions like tennis elbow or medial elbow pain, physical therapy is very important. A physical therapist may show you special exercises that stretch and strengthen your muscles and tendons around the elbow.

Sometimes, a doctor may use an injection to help calm the pain and swelling. When the pain is due to arthritis in the elbow, a doctor might follow a similar plan with rest, medicine, and physical therapy.
