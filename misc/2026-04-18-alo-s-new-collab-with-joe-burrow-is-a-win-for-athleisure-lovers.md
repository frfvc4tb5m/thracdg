BENGALS STAR QUARTERBACK Joe Burrow is known for his fiery on-field persona, and his latest collaboration with Alo Yoga seems to be geared towards helping us commonfolk stoke our own competitive flames.

The yoga brand just announced the Conquer Collection, its latest venture with the reigning NFL passing yards leader. The athletic apparel and lifestyle brand, renowned for its premium athleisure gear and focus on core tenets of wellness and mindfulness (its name is an acronym for air, land, and ocean), said in a statement that Burrow’s own skillset serves as inspiration for the collection’s prized pieces.

“Known for his calm under pressure, relentless drive, and unmatched mental toughness, Joe Burrow shows up when it counts, on and off the field,” Alo said on its site. “His approach mirrors the mindset behind every piece in this collection: focused, consistent, and built for the long game.”

Alo goes on to describe the collection as “pieces in a super-soft fabric that's designed for reaching peak performance.” The new lineup features everything from joggers to sleeveless tees to quarter-zip performance wear. The collection, the brand said, is geared specifically towards those with a similarly maniacal approach to preparation and detail as the 2x Comeback Player of the Year Award winner.

“Designed for the ones who show up early, stay late, and keep going long after the motivation fades, the Conquer collection is performance gear made for today, tomorrow, and beyond,” the company noted.

Alo and Burrow’s partnership began last September, when the Pro Bowler starred in an ad campaign rolling out the retailer's new line of running shoes. The Alo Runners marked the Alo's entryway into luxury performance footwear, with the signal caller leading the helm. Burrow has also been recently spotted wearing some of the brand’s flagship styles in a pair of preseason games this year, a steep departure from his typical flashy pre-game attire.

The Conquer Collection campaign runs from now through September 19. In the meantime, take a look at some of our favorite styles, and you can shop the entire collection below.
