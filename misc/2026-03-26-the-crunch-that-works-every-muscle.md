If you’ve been hibernating like a grizzly all season, the bear crunch will wake up nearly every muscle in your body—and attack your abs! Here’s how to do it: Start on all fours, and raise your knees one inch off the ground. The next part is tricky—and what makes the move such an ab assault—so watch the video below to see how to do it correctly.

“The bear crunch integrates so many different things—it’s not just an ab burn,” says Jeremy Frisch, U.S.A.W., owner of Achieve Performance in Acton, Massachusetts, and creator of the 24-Hour Arms Workout. “If you don’t really focus on keeping your position, especially when you try to rotate, you can fall right down. You really have to contract all those muscles from your fingers to your toes to be able to keep the crunch together.” For a core circuit you can do before your workout, Frisch says to combine the bear crunch with the crab hip hold. (Click here to see how to do the crab hip hold.) Do 10 reps of the bear crunch and10 reps of the crab hip hold, resting 30 seconds after each set. Repeat the circuit two to three times.
