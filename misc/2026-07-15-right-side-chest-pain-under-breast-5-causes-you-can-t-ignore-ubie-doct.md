What could be causing chest pain on the right side under the breast?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Right-side chest pain under the breast is often caused by chest wall conditions such as costochondritis (inflammation of the rib cartilage) or muscle strain from overuse or injury. In some cases, the discomfort may stem from breast tissue itself, including hormonal changes, cysts, or mastitis. Less commonly, it can signal issues involving the lungs, gallbladder, or digestive system, so persistent or severe pain should not be ignored.

[Chest Pain Under Breast](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/7421835460.html)

When you feel pain on the right side of your chest just under the breast, it may be due to a problem with the structures outside of your heart. One common cause is costochondritis. This happens when the cartilage that connects your ribs to your breastbone becomes inflamed, and it often causes sharp or aching pain in a specific spot. Muscle strain from physical activities or injuries can also result in chest wall pain in that area.

Another possibility is that the pain comes from the breast tissue itself. Changes in breast tissue, whether related to hormonal shifts or benign conditions, can sometimes be felt as pain under the breast. It is also possible that issues like trauma or even nerve irritation could be contributing to the discomfort.

Because chest pain can have many causes, it is a good idea to get personalized guidance.
