Ashwagandha (Withania somnifera) is an adaptogenic herb long used in Ayurvedic medicine. In recent years, scientific studies have explored its potential benefits for male fertility. This article reviews how doctors and researchers use Ashwagandha to improve sperm count and quality, based on credible sources, without creating unnecessary anxiety. Understanding Ashwagandha and Male Fertility

Ashwagandha is prized for its stress-reducing, antioxidant, and hormone-modulating properties. In the context of male fertility, these actions can support:

Hormone balance: Encouraging healthy testosterone levels

Oxidative stress reduction: Protecting sperm from free-radical damage

Improved semen parameters: Enhancing sperm count, motility, and morphology

By addressing both hormonal and cellular factors, Ashwagandha and male fertility appear interconnected through multiple pathways.

Key Mechanisms of Action

Researchers believe Ashwagandha helps male reproductive health via:

Reduces cortisol (the stress hormone)

Supports adrenal function, indirectly boosting reproductive hormones

Increases levels of superoxide dismutase (SOD) and catalase

Clinical Evidence

Several clinical trials provide insight into Ashwagandha's effects on sperm health:

Population: Men aged 20–40 with mild infertility

Dosage: 5 g/day root powder for 90 days

19% improvement in motility

Population: Infertile men with oligospermia

Dosage: 675 mg/day standardized extract for 12 weeks

17% increase in sperm concentration

11% improvement in vitality

Significant reduction in oxidative stress markers

While sample sizes are modest, results consistently favor Ashwagandha over placebo for key semen parameters.

Typical Dosage and Forms

Doctors may recommend different preparations, depending on patient needs:

3–6 grams daily, divided into 2–3 doses

300–600 mg twice daily

Contain concentrated extract for consistent dosing

Starting with a lower dose and gradually increasing helps monitor tolerance and side effects.

Safety and Side Effects

Ashwagandha is generally well tolerated, but doctors watch for:

Gastrointestinal upset: Mild nausea or diarrhea

Drowsiness: Especially when taken at higher doses

Thyroid effects: May increase thyroid hormone levels in sensitive individuals

Precautions:

Avoid if you have an autoimmune thyroid disorder without medical supervision.

Stop use and consult your doctor if you experience severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, or allergic reactions.

Interactions and Contraindications

Ashwagandha may interact with:

Sedatives or sleep aids: Can enhance drowsiness

Thyroid medications: Potential additive effects

Immunosuppressants: May stimulate immune activity

Always review your current medication list with a healthcare provider before starting Ashwagandha.
