# 5 Standing Abs Exercises to Build a Strong Core Without Going to the Ground

[Exercises to Build Strong](https://synaptide-lab.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/2467777589.html)

If your idea of an ab workout only requires you hit the floor for endless crunches, planks, and hollow holds, you should probably reassess your training plan. For one, there are plenty of exercises that help to build a strong and stable core without getting on the ground that you might not even think are ab crushers—heavy loaded carries, for example. But you can also train your abs with little or no load on your feet using similar movements that you would typically try on a mat.

[Core Without](https://neurocurrent.china-akan.com/)

This 5 exercise series from trainer Charlee Atkins, C.S.C.S., gives you a chance to build stronger abs without going to the ground. The movements reinforce core stability—if you perform them with strict form, emphasizing a core squeeze and a straight, safe spinal position. That means you don't round your back, so slow down if you feel yourself starting to slip.
