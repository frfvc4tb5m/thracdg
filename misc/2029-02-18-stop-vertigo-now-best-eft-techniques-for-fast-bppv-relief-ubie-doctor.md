Which Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) help with Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV)?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Emotional Freedom Techniques (EFT) have not been shown to treat Benign Paroxysmal Positional Vertigo (BPPV). BPPV is a mechanical inner-ear condition caused by displaced calcium crystals in the semicircular canals, and it is most effectively managed through proven repositioning maneuvers such as the Epley or Semont maneuver, not through tapping-based therapies like EFT.
