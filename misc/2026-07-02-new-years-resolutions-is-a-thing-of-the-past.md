Create Life Goals & Not New Year Resolutions

Grab a notebook and make this your “life book”. In our modern age, we have lost the art of writing and it’s time to get it back. Putting a pen to a piece of paper is therapeutic and solidifies our thoughts and ideas. Writing down your Life Goals will help you stay focused and actually execute your plans for the upcoming year. Life Goals is just the building block to create a routine in your life – it takes approximately 66 days to build a neuro-chemical pathway in your brain to maintain a routine.

Big grand goals are great to write down to try to accomplish them 1 by 1. But uber successful individuals take it a step further and break down each goal into actionable steps to actually achieve their goals. Its the art of focusing on 1 or 2 important prioritized tasks throughout your day to move you one step closer to your goals and vision. It is important to break down the big goals into smaller short term goals to give you a bit of structure and strategy.

Now, lets take it one step further and lets obtain your goals within the next 3 months. Learning from the great, Tim Ferris & his book “The 4 Hour Work Week” – it is time to optimize your time and really focus on what is most important to you. Tim is all about optimizing your time spent on “work” so it can free up your time to actually do what you love – the things that bring you absolute joy and bliss. Mine is traveling and adventurous trips.
