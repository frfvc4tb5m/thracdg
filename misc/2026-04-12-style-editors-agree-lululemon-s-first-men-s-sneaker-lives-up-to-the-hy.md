# Style Editors Agree: Lululemon's First Men's Sneaker Lives Up to the Hype

[Lululemon's First Men's Sneaker](https://hostdefense-journal.escortskhi.com/blog/2237607506.html)

Welcome to MH Style Gains, where Men’s Health's Style Team finds the best new menswear releases of the month so you can look and feel your best.

LOOKING TO REFRESH your fits this Spring? You're in luck. Some of our favorite menswear brands have been dropping some seriously sharp duds that can be worn just about anywhere this season. We're talking handsome-improving shades from Oliver Peoples, Lululemon's sleek everyday sneakers, and Ben Sherman's layering essential track jacket. While these clothes, watches, and shoes are part of brand new collections, these aren't flash-in-the-plan pieces. Our fashion team says they're eternally stylish, can dress up any fit, and are meant to be worn for a lifetime, trends be damned. Read on for our favorite April menswear releases.

Brooks Brothers Vintage-Inspired Rugby

Brooks Brothers has been churning out some seriously great knitwear, with a twist. Take this '80s-inspired rugby shirt, which has a woven oxford shirt collar that's a nod to the brand's rich tailoring heritage. Our fashion team loves the ultra-sueded cotton, which is thick, durable, and fits well. We recommend throwing it on during a midday sports event, or chilly beach parties.

Oliver Peoples x Roger Federer Sunglasses

Tennis GOAT Roger Federer linked up with Oliver Peoples for these champion-level shades. The lightweight bio-nylon frame can take a licking, and moves easily on and off the tennis court. Bonus points for the performance lenses that help reduce squinting. "These are a smart investment if you're looking for a flattering pair of sunglasses that look as well as it performs," says MH fashion director Ted Stafford.

Lululemon Cityverse Sneaker

After much hype, Lululemon finally dropped its first men's shoe, and it's pretty awesome. The athleisure giant developed a casual lace-up that's loaded with performance, including a grippy outsole to prevent rainy day slip-ups, and a contoured insole that hits the right balance of comfort and stability. While we wouldn't recommend it as a workout shoe, it is a comfortable sneaker for standing (or walking) all day in.

[Lululemon's First](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/)

J.Crew Performance polo shirt with Coolmax

Golf season is in full swing, and reliable J.Crew dropped a classic polo shirt to sport on and off the links. It has a hint of stretch that moves like a second skin during our swings, and its moisture-wicking fabric will keep you feeling fresh on those unusually warm days. Plus, we have to talk about the fit. It's slim, but not tight, and flatters most body types. We like most of the solid colors, but we prefer the striped style.

Buck Mason Herringbone Twill Pant

Meet your new spring slacks. Buck Mason developed a military-style pant and put it through a unique rinsing and dying process. The result: a twill trouser that's tough, yet stylish enough for the office. Our fashion team says its higher rise and straight leg plays well with a variety of looks, including your favorite T-shirt and bomber jacket.

Mack Weldon AirKnit Boxer Brief

Time after time, we've praised Mack Weldon as one of the best makers of men's underwear, and its latest Airknit boxer briefs are no exception. It has the brand's proprietary breathable fabric that wicks away moisture, so you can avoid swamp crotch during your workouts, or hot commutes. "The new bright colors will uplift your warm weather style from within," says Stafford.

Banana Republic Diego Suede Chelsea Boot

We love a great Chelsea boot since it's easy to slip on and off, and dressy enough to wear to most business casual and cocktail events. This pale suede pair has a leather lining that's pretty comfortable compared to other Chelseas we tried. And at this price point, you're getting a great quality shoe for a bargain. Pair them with pale jeans, khakis, or dress pants for a warm weather uniform.

Ben Sherman Signature Tape Track Jacket

[Lives Up to](https://nootropic-atlas.escortskhi.com/)

Ben Sherman is a UK brand that knows how make spring layers. Enter this track jacket, which is lightweight, and has a stretch fabric that moves easily, while still providing a bit of insulation for those chilly spring mornings. Style-wise, you can't go wrong with the striped sleeves that add a collegiate flair to your fits. "It's cool, sporty, and the perfect layering piece for guys on the go," says Stafford.

Timex x Todd Snyder Q 1978 Watch

Todd Snyder rose through the menswear ranks for its elevated-but-still-approachable pieces that can be worn just about anywhere. Case in point: this sharp ticker with Timex. It has a two-tone design that adds a subtle pop to your wrist game, and lends a classy-casual vibe that looks great at work, or a night out. "It's a timelessly cool style at a value well worth adding to your watch rotation," says Stafford.
