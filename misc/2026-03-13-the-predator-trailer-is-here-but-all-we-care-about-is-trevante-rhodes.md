# 'The Predator' Trailer Is Here, But All We Care About Is Trevante Rhodes Firing a Machine Gun

The first trailer for Shane Black's The Predator dropped on Thursday, giving the world a peek at the updated take on the 1987 Arnold Schwarzenegger-starring, alien-hunts-humans classic.

We meet the stacked cast, including Sterling K. Brown, Olivia Munn, and Keegan-Michael Key, get a few glimpses at the updated look of the apex Predator itself, and quite frankly none of this matters next to the 0.2-second-long clip of a jacked Trevante Rhodes sitting atop a giant machine gun:

What a mesmerizing image that is. I am sold on this film. Sold. I just bought a dozen tickets, and they're not even on sale yet. I hope the movie is just 30 minutes long and ends with the Predators turning their ship around immediately, returning to space with stories about how the human race has never once skipped tricep day. The rest of the movie can be an unbroken shot of Rhodes doing dips off the machine gun, I don't care.

The actor, of course, is probably best known for his star-making turn in the Oscar-winning Moonlight. Told over three segments of the same life, Barry Jenkins' coming of age film tells the story of a closeted gay black man named Chiron — Rhodes plays Chiron as a young adult — finding his place in the world. It's a gorgeous film — stylistically, visually, and tonally — that more than earned its Best Picture victory.

Reactions to the Predator trailer have been mixed so far — phrases like "totally meh" are being thrown around about a teaser that "features way more Jacob Tremblay than most people are expecting" — and a lot of that has to with the rebooted story. Where the original Predator followed a squad of U.S. Army special forces into the Val Verde jungle, Black's take seems to have the race of super-aliens crash landing in a suburb because a little boy somehow stole one of their helmets. That is...admittedly dumb!

[Trailer Is Here](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

But people, don't forget about Trevante. I urge you, do not forget about Trevante. More than anything, that brief glimpse of Trevante Rhodes showcases what the true spirit of the Predator franchise has been since the very beginning: Absurdly muscular dudes wildly firing machine guns into the jungle for almost no reason.

The Predator premieres September 14. Prepare your triceps accordingly.
