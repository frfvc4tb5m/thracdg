# Our Fashion Team Found the Best Menswear Releases of October

Welcome to MH Style Gains, where the Men’s Health style team finds the best new menswear releases of the month so you can look and feel your best.

FALL CAN BE a tricky time to figure out your fits, given the season’s unpredictable weather. Do you ditch the layers during an unreasonably hot afternoon? Or keep them on hand in case the temp drops in the evening? Thankfully, our style team has you covered with its shortlist of the best new menswear releases this October. Fashion director Ted Stafford combed the market to find the latest pieces that are functional, work for all sorts of occasions, and look great with just about everything in your fall closet. He landed on over a dozen handsome staples (think hoodies, chinos, and fleece jackets), with guy-ready features like moisture-wicking material and stretchy fabrics to keep you warm, dry, and comfortable when you’re on the move. Read on for our 14 favorite new clothing drops of the month.

Dockers Go Chino

“When you think of America’s favorite chino, the only right answer is Dockers,” says Stafford. That’s because for nearly 40 years the brand has been making top-notch pants that are built to look and feel great. Take its new Go Chino, which features the brand’s new Airweave fabric: a stretchy, eco-friendly polyester with proprietary 4-way stretch and Cool To Touch technology designed to wick away moisture and keep you cool or warm, depending on what you need. In other words, perfect for fall weather. Stafford especially loves the non-wrinkle fabric, meaning you can easily throw these on, spend all day working or dashing around doing errands, and still look put-together.

New Balance Made in USA 993

The OG dad-shoe brand has come up with a fall-ready colorway for its classic Made in USA 993 silhouette, and the result is fantastic. The brown suede upper has sleek purple detailing, which adds a cool (but not obnoxious) contrast. Plus, the brand’s famed midsole cushion is so comfortable, you could easily work, stand, and walk in these all day.

Todd Snyder x Woolrich Sherpa Fleece Jacket

“The new Woolrich x Todd Snyder collection is a winning combination of a classic heritage brand and a modern American menswear designer,” says Stafford. I mean, look at the results in this outstanding sherpa fleece jacket. Velcro patch pockets give it a striking contrast, and the raglan sleeves and windbreak collar provide extra insulation. And did we mention the feel of the sherpa? It’s super soft and relatively lightweight, so you’ll stay cozy during all your outdoor autumn adventures.

Dockers Go Tee

[Our Fashion Team](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/0195527333.html)

Here at Men’s Health, we’re always on the hunt for the perfect tee. Enter this promising contender from Dockers, which Stafford calls the quintessential long-sleeve T-shirt for fall layering. That’s because it’s designed with a slim but not tight cut that makes it easy to wear under a shirt, vest, or coat. Meanwhile, the mesh panels help regulate temperature and moisture to keep you warm and dry.

Uniqlo JW Anderson Souffle Yarn Ribbed Zip Sweater

JW Anderson and Uniqlo just released their fall/winter 2024 collection, and our style team says this ribbed sweater is a necessity for winter. We love its “Souffle Yarn” fabric, which is super soft and silky yet also light and durable. That means it’ll hold its shape and easily move with you whether you’re raking leaves or shopping for the holidays. “This piece looks great with a chino pant and tee for an elevated casual look,” says Stafford.

Swatch Skinearth Watch

Swatch’s latest Skinearth watch is sleek and lightweight yet built to take a licking. That’s because it has a stainless-steel case that can withstand scratches and dents. Plus, its sun-brushed dial and bright orange hour indices make it easy to tell the time in all sorts of conditions. We love the textured olive strap, which is perfect for fall and doubles down on the watch’s outdoor vibe.

Dockers Work Shirt

Meet your casual Friday fit. Dockers’ work shirt is both functional and handsome, thanks to durable denim that holds its shape well. Meanwhile, the medium wash rinse with detailed stitching is eternally stylish and looks great with all your laid-back fits. We especially recommend pairing this with chinos for a smart casual look or with joggers for an elevated active look.

Eddie Bauer Reissue Down Bomber Jacket

Eddie Bauer updates its OG bomber jacket with a leaner and meaner silhouette. Its 650 down fill is lightweight yet provides supreme insulation, and its StormRepel fabric does a bang-up job of keeping the elements at bay. Whether you wear it straight or cinched at the waist, our style team says this is the ideal jacket for transitioning from fall to winter.

Cole Haan GrandPrø Tennis Sneakers

If there’s one thing (aside from its legendary loafer, of course) that Cole Haan does really well, it’s a classic simple sneaker. What makes this GrandPrø shoe so good is that it’s got a perfectly cushioned insole and a perfectly grippy outsole to keep your feet comfortable and planted, whether you’re at the office or on the tennis court. Its versatility and lightweight build make this a superb everyday shoe.

Lululemon Steady State Pullover Hoodie

One of our favorite activewear brands, Lululemon recently dropped its editor-tested Steady State hoodie in a bunch of fall-ready colors. We’re partial to this chocolate brown option, which will look great with all of your autumn fits. Plus, it has a breathable cotton fleece that will keep you warm without losing your cool.

Everlane ReNew Transit Backpack

Looking for a lightweight, travel-friendly backpack? The Everlane ReNew Transit backpack is your guy. Built to carry a weekend’s worth of essentials, it also has a water-resistant shell that doesn’t add a lot of bulk, making it ideal for business trips or daily commutes. We also like its easy-to-access laptop pocket, along with a pass-through strap that seamlessly attaches to your carry-on luggage. “We fully endorse this as a must-have for holiday travels,” Stafford says.

As the mercury starts to dip, a good polo sweater is an essential wardrobe staple for fall. And Dockers has just what you need with this classic option: It’s comfortable and flattering with a stylish, tailored fit.

American Eagle Timberland x AE Anorak Jacket

American Eagle has teamed up with Timberland to bring you a cool capsule collection just in time for fall. Combining iconic Timberland pieces with American Eagle’s style, the collection includes heavyweight crewneck sweatshirts and soft fleece sweats, but a favorite of ours is this rust-orange anorak, a go-to layering piece for the season. The front kangaroo pouch offers easy access to your on-the-go items and doubles as a hand warmer for those chilly fall hikes.

Dockers Go Jogger

Finding a pair of joggers that feel comfortable but look elevated isn’t easy. That’s why Dockers’ newly designed Go Jogger is a big win. It features the brand’s temperature-regulating fabric to keep your legs feeling great all day. And we love the slim cut, which looks polished but isn’t too tight. Stafford recommends wearing these with your everyday staples or with a blazer for a dressier (but still sporty!) look.
