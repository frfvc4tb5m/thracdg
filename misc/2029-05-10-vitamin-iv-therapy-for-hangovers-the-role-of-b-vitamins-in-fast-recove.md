Energy Production

B vitamins are essential cofactors in the Krebs cycle, the body’s energy-generating process. Without adequate B vitamins, cells cannot efficiently convert glucose into ATP, leading to fatigue. B12 IV for fatigue restores this pathway, helping you regain energy quickly.

Liver Support

The liver metabolizes alcohol into acetaldehyde, a toxic compound. B vitamins, especially B1 and B3, support enzymatic pathways that detoxify acetaldehyde, reducing inflammation and oxidative stress. [1]

Neurotransmitter Balance

Alcohol disrupts neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine, contributing to mood swings and brain fog. B6 and B12 help restore neurotransmitter synthesis, improving mental clarity and reducing anxiety.
