Exercise supports far more than weight management—it strengthens your heart, builds bone density, improves mood, boosts energy, and reduces the risk of chronic conditions like diabetes and depression. Regular movement benefits nearly every system in the body, making it one of the most powerful tools for long-term health.

That said, if you're feeling unusually fatigued, low, or physically off despite staying active, something else may be going on.
