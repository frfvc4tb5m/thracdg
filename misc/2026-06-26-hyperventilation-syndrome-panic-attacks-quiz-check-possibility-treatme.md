What is Hyperventilation Syndrome / Panic Attacks?

An anxiety disorder in which a person has repeated and often unexpected panic attacks. These panic attacks involve a sudden onset of intense fearfulness in the absence of danger and includes physical symptoms such as heart palpitations, difficulty breathing, chest pain or discomfort, choking or smothering sensations, sweating, and dizziness.

Typical Symptoms of Hyperventilation Syndrome / Panic Attacks

Tremors of hands and body

Fear of suffocation or death when having difficulty breathing

Diagnostic Questions for Hyperventilation Syndrome / Panic Attacks

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing tremors in your hands or body?

Do you experience sudden, intense anxiety or fear?

Do you feel fear of suffocation or death when you have difficulty breathing?

Do you think your fears are sometimes irrational or illogical?

Do you have numbness in your lower legs?

Treatment of Hyperventilation Syndrome / Panic Attacks

There are both medication and psychotherapy options to help with panic disorder. Exposure and Response Prevention based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy is particularly well known to help.
