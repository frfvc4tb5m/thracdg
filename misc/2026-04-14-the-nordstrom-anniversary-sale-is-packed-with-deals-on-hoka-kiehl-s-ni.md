Invest in Your Grooming Routine

My personal favorite deals to look at are always in the skincare department. Listen, that stuff is expensive and runs out faster than anything else on this list, so you can’t blame a guy for wanting to stock up while he can. Kiehl’s is one of my go-to brands, especially when it comes to its cleansers and moisturizers, both of which are seeing discounts up to 35 percent off. I always lean toward Le Labo when I’m feeling more boujee than I actually am, and so you can put money on me taking the shower gel off the shelves. Supergoop is also saving the day with a duo pack of its SPF. I’ll die on the hill that you need to wear sunscreen every day if you want to preserve your skin, so this should be your first purchase if you’re anything like me.

Expand Your Athleisure

With sales on athletic gear like Hoka sneakers, Nike running shorts, On running shoes, and more, Nordstrom is making your fitness goals a top priority.
