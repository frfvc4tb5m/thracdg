When May’s first product, the Modern Day Briefcase, debuted on Kickstarter it raised over $240,000. His immediate success can easily be credited to his sharp eye for detail. The Modern Day Briefcase (essentially a backpack on steroids) is fitted with padded shoulder straps, internal pockets for pens and credit cards, and a secure sleeve for your laptop. May’s other products come with similar features i.e. a protective laptop sleeve and pockets for all your gadgets.

The Breton Company is indicative of a new generation of entrepreneurs. Small brands looking for exposure are turning to Kickstarter, or similar websites, to allow the everydayman to identify a problem or modern day inconvenience and then create a tangible solution.
