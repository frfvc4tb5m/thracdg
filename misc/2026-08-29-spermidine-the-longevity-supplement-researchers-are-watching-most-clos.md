What Is Spermidine?

Spermidine is a naturally occurring polyamine found in all living cells.

It plays a key role in cell growth, gene regulation, and protein synthesis.

As we age, our bodies produce less spermidine, which may impact the efficiency of cellular housekeeping processes.

How Spermidine Supports Longevity

Researchers believe spermidine longevity benefits stem primarily from its ability to boost autophagy, the body's way of cleaning out damaged cells and regenerating newer, healthier ones.

Autophagy Activation• Helps remove damaged proteins and organelles• Supports healthy cell turnover• May reduce inflammation linked to aging

Mitochondrial Health• Improves energy production in cells• Protects against oxidative stress

Cardiovascular Protection• May support healthy blood pressure and arterial function• Could lower the risk of age-related heart issues

Safety and Dosage

Typical Dose: 1–3 mg spermidine daily

Side Effects: Rare and usually mild (upset stomach, headache)

Interactions: No major drug interactions documented as of 2026, but always check with your healthcare provider

Long-term safety data in humans is still accumulating. Current evidence suggests spermidine is well tolerated, even in older adults.
