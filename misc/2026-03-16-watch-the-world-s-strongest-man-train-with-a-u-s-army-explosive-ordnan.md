# Watch the World's Strongest Man Train With a U.S. Army Explosive Ordnance Disposal Team

To make it in the U.S. military, you have to be fit. But there are specialized branches and teams within the branches of the service, so standards vary. World's Strongest Man Martins Licis headed to Fort Knox to do some training with a squad that doesn't get as much attention as some of the others: the U.S. Army's Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) team.

[Strongest Man Train With](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

Once he's there, SSG Jake Hankins explains the suit they wear from protection to protect them from blasts and shrapnel. The large suit that Martins will wear during his training wears 90 pounds.

"I was asking them about survivability. If one pound of TNT is five feet away, you can survive. Anything closer, you're pretty much screwed," says Licis. "But it's about the approach, because a bomb can go off at any moment during the approach."

Licis gets suited up in the black and green protective gear.

"Is it like The Hurt Locker?" asks Licis' friend Romark. He is assured it very much is not like the movies.

In the final round, the race is neck-and-neck, but is halted when one of the members carrying a bomb trips and the 100+ pound artillery round lands on his hand, which was clearly very painful.

"That's the risk of being in the EOD, as you can see," says Licis. "In addition to the weight of the suit, these guys are absolute bad asses. To go through all of that conditioning and still be able to stand up."
