Explanation

Finasteride is a medication that can affect PSA (prostate-specific antigen) levels. PSA is a protein produced by the prostate gland, and its levels are often checked to screen for prostate issues.

Effects of Finasteride

Lowered PSA Levels: According to the studies, finasteride can significantly reduce PSA levels. This means that if you are taking finasteride, your PSA test results might be lower than they would be without the medication.

Adjustment Needed: Doctors often adjust PSA test results for patients taking finasteride to get an accurate understanding of prostate health.
