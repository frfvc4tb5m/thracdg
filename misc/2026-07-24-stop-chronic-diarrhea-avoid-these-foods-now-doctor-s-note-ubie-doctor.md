Which foods should be avoided for those with chronic diarrhea?

Internal Medicine

Chronic diarrhea is often worsened by high-sugar foods, including sugary drinks, candy, pastries, and processed snacks. Sugars like fructose and sugar alcohols (sorbitol, mannitol) can draw water into the intestines, increasing stool frequency and looseness. Limiting these foods may help reduce flare-ups and support digestive recovery.

Because chronic diarrhea can stem from many underlying causes—ranging from food intolerances and IBS to infections or inflammatory conditions—understanding what's driving your symptoms is essential to finding relief. Explanation

Chronic diarrhea can be uncomfortable and disruptive. To help manage it, certain foods should be avoided. According to the provided references, foods high in sugar can negatively impact gastrointestinal health. Sugars can cause the body to draw more water into the intestines, which can make diarrhea worse.

Here are some tips:

Avoid sugary foods and drinks: Sodas, candies, and desserts often contain high amounts of sugar.

Limit artificial sweeteners: These can also lead to digestive issues.
